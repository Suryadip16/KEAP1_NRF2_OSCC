# Methodology: NRF2 Gene Set Version Benchmarking

This document details the analytical methodology used in the `03a_geneset_version_comparison.R` script to evaluate and benchmark five candidate NRF2 core gene sets.

## 1. Analytical Pipeline

The script employs a systematic approach to determine which gene set version provides the optimal separation for downstream Differential Gene Expression Analysis (DGEA).

1. **Data Preprocessing**: The script ingests the Variance Stabilizing Transformation (VST) normalized RNA-seq count matrix and clinical metadata. It extracts the 5 candidate gene lists (V0 through V4) and maps them against the available genes in the expression matrix.
2. **Pathway Scoring**: For each of the 5 gene set versions, pathway activity scores are calculated across all samples using two distinct algorithms, resulting in 10 unique scoring runs:
   - **GSVA (Gene Set Variation Analysis)**: Uses a Gaussian kernel density estimation (appropriate for continuous, normally-distributed VST data) to assess relative pathway enrichment.
   - **ssGSEA (Single-Sample GSEA)**: Uses a rank-based empirical cumulative distribution function to calculate enrichment.
3. **Group Classification**: The analysis is restricted to tumor samples. For each of the 10 scoring runs, a median split is applied to the tumor scores, categorizing samples into `NRF2-High` and `NRF2-Low` groups.
4. **Metric Evaluation & Ranking**: 7 quantitative metrics (detailed below) are computed for each run. These metrics are normalized (0 to 1 scale) and aggregated into a weighted **Composite Score** to definitively rank the performance of each gene set version and scoring method combination.

## 2. Evaluation Metrics

The benchmarking relies on 7 distinct metrics designed to evaluate both the statistical robustness and biological validity of the classification.

### 2.1 Bimodality Coefficient (Sarle's BC)
* **What it is**: A statistical calculation based on skewness and excess kurtosis that quantifies the likelihood of a distribution having two distinct peaks (bimodal) rather than one (unimodal). Values > 0.555 generally indicate bimodality.
* **Why it matters here**: Constitutive NRF2 activation in OSCC is typically driven by specific somatic alterations (e.g., *KEAP1* loss-of-function or *NFE2L2* gain-of-function mutations) present in a distinct subpopulation of tumors. Therefore, the pathway scores across the cohort *should* exhibit a bimodal distribution, reflecting the underlying biological reality of NRF2-active versus NRF2-inactive tumor states.

### 2.2 Silhouette Width
* **What it is**: A measure of cluster cohesion and separation, ranging from -1 to +1. In this context, it measures how similar a tumor's score is to others in its assigned group (High or Low) compared to tumors in the opposing group.
* **Why it matters here**: Evaluates the cleanliness of the median split. A higher average silhouette width indicates that the `NRF2-High` and `NRF2-Low` groups form distinct, well-separated populations with minimal boundary overlap, confirming that the median is cutting through a true valley in the data.

### 2.3 Cohen's d (Effect Size)
* **What it is**: A standardized measure of the difference between two group means, calculated as the absolute difference between the `NRF2-High` and `NRF2-Low` mean scores divided by their pooled standard deviation.
* **Why it matters here**: This is perhaps the most critical metric for the pipeline's ultimate goal. A larger effect size indicates a massive magnitude of separation between the two groups. Stronger separation directly translates to greater statistical power and cleaner signal in the subsequent downstream Differential Gene Expression Analysis (DGEA).

### 2.4 NRF2 Target Validation
* **What it is**: The mean of the $-log_{10}$ transformed p-values derived from one-sided Wilcoxon rank-sum tests. These tests evaluate the differential expression of 6 canonical, literature-established NRF2 direct targets (*NQO1*, *HMOX1*, *GCLC*, *SLC7A11*, *GPX2*, *TXNRD1*) between the High and Low groups.
* **Why it matters here**: This serves as the biological ground truth. Regardless of mathematical clustering, a valid NRF2 gene set must successfully classify tumors such that known, canonical NRF2 downstream targets are significantly overexpressed in the `NRF2-High` group.

### 2.5 GSVA-ssGSEA Concordance
* **What it is**: The Spearman rank correlation coefficient ($\rho$) between the scores generated by the GSVA method and those generated by the ssGSEA method for a specific gene set version.
* **Why it matters here**: Assesses algorithmic robustness. High concordance indicates that the biological signal defined by the gene set is strong and intrinsic, allowing it to transcend the mathematical differences between different scoring methodologies.

### 2.6 Biological Coherence
* **What it is**: The mean pairwise Spearman correlation among all individual genes within a specific gene set, calculated across all tumor samples.
* **Why it matters here**: Measures the internal consistency of the gene set. Moderate coherence is ideal; it confirms the genes are co-regulated by the same transcriptional program. Extremely high values suggest redundancy (genes providing duplicate information), while very low values indicate the inclusion of uncorrelated "noise" genes that dilute the overarching pathway signal.

### 2.7 Dynamic Range (IQR)
* **What it is**: The Interquartile Range (IQR) of the pathway scores, measuring the spread of the middle 50% of the data.
* **Why it matters here**: A wider spread indicates better discriminatory power across the tumor cohort. It ensures the median split is dividing a truly dispersed dataset into distant groups, rather than arbitrarily halving a dense, homogenous cluster.

## 3. Composite Scoring Framework

To select the absolute best gene set, the 7 metrics are synthesized into a single composite score. 

First, metrics are normalized to a 0-1 scale. For Biological Coherence, a penalty is applied to extremes (both too high and too low), targeting a moderate optimal value (0.25). 

A weighted average is then calculated, with weights assigned based on analytical priority for this specific OSCC pipeline:
1. **Target Validation (25%)**: Biological ground truth is paramount.
2. **Cohen's d (20%)**: Directly dictates power for the downstream DGEA.
3. **Silhouette Width (20%)**: Ensures clean group separation.
4. **Bimodality (15%)**: Ensures the scores reflect the expected mutated/wild-type subpopulations.
5. **Dynamic Range (10%)**: Rewards broader score distribution.
6. **Biological Coherence (10%)**: Penalizes noisy or overly redundant gene sets.

## 4. Purpose and Biological Rationale of NRF2 Target Validation Plots

The validation plots generated during benchmarking (`03a_geneset_target_validation.pdf` / `geneset_target_validation.png`) and final pathway scoring (`03_nrf2_target_expression_by_group.pdf` / `nrf2_target_expression_by_group.png`) compare the expression of a gold-standard panel of 6 canonical NRF2 direct transcriptional targets (*NQO1*, *HMOX1*, *GCLC*, *SLC7A11*, *GPX2*, *TXNRD1*) between `NRF2-High` and `NRF2-Low` tumours. 

These plots serve four primary purposes within the analytical framework:

### 4.1 Establishing Biological Ground Truth vs. Mathematical Artefact
* **Unsupervised Clustering Risk**: Pathway enrichment scoring algorithms (GSVA, ssGSEA) and median-split stratifications are purely mathematical operations. Any dataset can mathematically be partitioned into two clusters, even if the division is driven by technical noise, batch effects, or irrelevant variance.
* **ARE-Driven Verification**: Every gene in the 6-target panel contains well-characterized Antioxidant Response Elements (AREs) in its promoter and represents universally recognized direct transcriptional effectors of NRF2 (Taguchi & Yamamoto, *Free Radic Biol Med* 2017).
* **Ground Truth Assurance**: Observing significant, coordinated upregulation of these 6 canonical targets confirms that tumors assigned to the `NRF2-High` group genuinely possess active in vivo NRF2 transcriptional programs, grounding statistical clustering in true cellular biology.

### 4.2 Quantitative Benchmarking of Gene Set Versions (25% Weight)
* In `03a_geneset_version_comparison.R`, target validation is quantified as the mean $-\log_{10}(p\text{-value})$ across one-sided Wilcoxon rank-sum tests for the 6 target genes.
* This metric carries the **highest individual weight (25%)** in the composite scoring framework because biological validity is a non-negotiable prerequisite.
* The multi-panel version validation plot (`03a_geneset_target_validation.pdf`) visualizes target expression across all 5 candidate gene sets (V0–V4), verifying that candidate modifications (e.g., adding CUL3/MAFs, removing ambiguous genes) maintain or improve biological separation.

### 4.3 Validating Key Functional Phenotypes in OSCC
The selected 6-gene panel specifically reflects the hallmark phenotypes under investigation in oral squamous cell carcinoma:
* **Prototypical NRF2 Marker (*NQO1*)**: Shows the most dramatic separation ($\Delta \approx 1.5$ VST units, $\approx 2.8$-fold on a linear scale), providing unambiguous confirmation of NRF2 pathway hyperactivation.
* **Ferroptosis Resistance (*SLC7A11*, *GPX2*)**: *SLC7A11* (catalytic subunit of system $\text{x}_c^-$ cystine/glutamate antiporter) and *GPX2* (glutathione peroxidase 2) mediate cystine uptake and lipid hydroperoxide clearance. Their prominent upregulation validates that the stratification captures the ferroptosis-resistant OSCC subpopulation.
* **Glutathione Biosynthesis & Chemoresistance (*GCLC*)**: *GCLC* catalyzes the rate-limiting step of glutathione biosynthesis, a primary driver of resistance to cisplatin and standard chemotherapeutics in OSCC.
* **Redox Homeostasis & Stress Adaptation (*HMOX1*, *TXNRD1*)**: Heme oxygenase-1 and thioredoxin reductase-1 reflect the tumor's amplified capacity to neutralize reactive oxygen species (ROS) and survive oxidative stress.

### 4.4 Essential Quality Gate for Downstream Differential Expression (DGEA)
* Prior to executing genome-wide Differential Gene Expression Analysis (DESeq2) comparing NRF2-High vs. NRF2-Low groups, researchers must confirm the validity of the phenotypic contrast.
* In `03_nrf2_pathway_scoring.R`, the script applies an automated check confirming that all direct targets reach statistical significance ($p < 0.05$, with actual values achieving $p < 10^{-11}$).
* The resulting violin/boxplot publication figure (`03_nrf2_target_expression_by_group.pdf`) provides a clear, defensible quality gate that prevents circular reasoning or false biomarker discovery in subsequent downstream analyses.
